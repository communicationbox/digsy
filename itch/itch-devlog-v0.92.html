Chimeras used to be a shopping list: bring an identified skull, ribcage and leg to the Lab, pay some coins and a DNA vial, done. It worked, but it also made the whole system pointless the moment you had the parts. There was no reason to ever wait for anything.

Chimeras now hatch, they don't get assembled

The instant-assembly button is gone. The only way to get a chimera now is to breed two creatures you already have, any two, chimeras or purely awakened species. Pick the two parents, then for the skull, the ribcage and the leg choose which parent it's inherited from: eight possible combinations per pair, a real decision instead of a dice roll.

The egg costs energy and your 3 least valuable duplicate fossils, a new use for duplicates besides selling and fusing them, and takes a couple of game-days to hatch. Feed it finer duplicates and the odds shift: a small chance of a related-species mutation, a small chance the offspring comes out a rarity higher than either parent.

A tank you can actually see

The egg used to only exist as a line of stats in a menu. Now there's a glass incubation tank standing in the Laboratory itself, next to the workbenches: empty and dim when there's nothing cooking, glowing with the egg floating inside once you've laid one. Walk in and you can tell at a glance whether something's brewing.

Also in this update

  • Variable market prices. What the Shop pays for each species drifts day to day, shown before you sell. Sometimes it pays to wait.
  • The park pays you back. While you're away, your chimeras draw visitors; come back to a few coins (and sometimes a surprise half-vial). Real time, capped at 10 hours, so vanishing for a week doesn't beat vanishing for a night.
  • Fixed the companion minigame's catch window landing half a second late. It now rings the instant it opens instead of after you've already missed it.
  • Fixed the Tailor: clicking the ✕ to go hatless left the Confirm button dead. Removing a hat is free, but the button was only counting paid changes.
