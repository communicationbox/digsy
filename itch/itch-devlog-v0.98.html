This is the biggest update since launch. It started as "the hats look a bit off" and ended with almost everything on screen redrawn, a second collection to chase, and far less text to read.

Amber: the collection keeps going

Finishing a Museum case used to be the end of that species: every duplicate after that was just coins. Now, once a case is complete, that species starts turning up as amber pieces ✨. Five of them light up an amber case, glowing orange among the gold ones, and it pays well. Rarer species drop amber more easily, so the legendaries don't become a wall. By my own pacing simulator, filling every amber case takes a few dozen hours on top of the main collection.

Awakening is a moment now

Bringing a species back used to be a line of text. Now it has its own little scene: the skeleton glows, there's a flash, and the living animal jumps out. And it goes straight to your garden, so the next time you're home it's there, walking around.

A letter hidden in the stone

Grandpa's letters used to start only after you filled a whole Museum room, hours in. The first one is now tucked behind the plaque of his statue, right next to the Museum. You can see the corner of the envelope. Just tap it.

A whole new look

  • Digsy, redrawn from scratch: new body, face and arms; hats and hairstyles drawn natively; four shirts that actually look different (T-shirt, tank top, shirt, hoodie).
  • Every animation redone: digging, chopping and mining now face the thing you hit, plus fishing, skates, bicycle (with a little basket) and a proper motorboat.
  • The flying mount is now a crystal dragon with real flapping wings, the same creature you see in the Book and your yard.
  • All 66 creatures polished: lively eyes, horns only where they belong, tapered snouts, hare ears, sturdier legs.
  • Towns come alive: the barber pole spins, chimneys smoke, awnings flutter in the wind, a rocking chair rocks outside the Furniture shop.
  • The world lost its grid: ragged edges between grass, forest, dirt and rock, shores and lakes with rounded corners, ten different dig holes.
  • New title menu with a little dig scene, a new intro, and a Bag that looks like a bag, a Map that looks like a map, a Book with a leather cover.

Less reading

Playtesters don't read, and they're right. Panels lost their paragraphs of explanation, descriptions are two or three words, and on phones the buttons are bigger and stay next to what they buy.

Also in this update

  • The map has a Teleport home button; a portal at home brings you back where you were.
  • A free Monstera for your home, and your companion now waits for you to leave the yard before you close the gate.
  • Thrown food no longer lands on the other side of the fence.
  • Autosave no longer causes hitches in very long games (and saves are much smaller).
  • Fixed white dashes appearing on land at the corners of water.
