Digsy World is a cozy pixel-art game about digging up the past. No combat, no losing, no rush — just wander, dig, and discover.

🪏 Dig up a forgotten world

You're Digsy, a small archaeologist in an endless, procedurally-generated world — golden meadows, bone deserts, ashen woods, red lands, ancient marshes and frozen wastes, each with creatures that live nowhere else. Dig anywhere. What comes out is a raw find: a lump of stone that could be anything. Carry it to the Museum and the experts will tell you what you brought them.

🏛️ A museum you walk through

The Museum is a building you explore: an entrance hall with the Curator's desk, a rotunda with a mounted skeleton, and six rooms — one per biome — lined with glass cases under their own spotlight. Hand over your finds, come back the next day, and what you found goes on display. Every species you complete lights up its rotating 3D voxel skeleton in your Fossil Book — and once a case is full, that species starts turning up as amber pieces ✨: a second collection to chase, glowing orange among the gold.

🥚 Bring creatures back — then make new ones

Collect enough DNA and a species comes back alive, walking around your yard. Put two of them together and they lay an egg: you choose which parent each part is inherited from, feed it the duplicates you don't need, and wait a couple of days. When it's ready the shell wobbles, cracks spread, and a brand-new chimera with a name of its own jumps out.

✉️ Your grandfather got here first

He left the Museum his life's work, and he left you letters. The first is hidden behind the plaque of his statue; the others wait in the rooms you fill, one per biome. They're the reason the Museum matters — and the reason you keep digging.

🛋️ A house to make your own

You get a house with four rooms and almost nothing in them. The Furniture shop sells a new selection every day — 12 themes, over 250 pieces, each one drawn by hand — plus wallpaper and flooring, which change a room more than any sofa. Put down rugs, stand furniture on them, hang paintings on the wall. It isn't just decoration: a room that feels lived-in gives you well rested in the morning, and sleeping in your own bed is free — no more paying the inn.

🏕️ A world with things in it

Towns with a fountain, shops, an inn, a barber and a tailor. Treasure maps with an X that always pays off, dark caves holding six species found nowhere else, 18 wonders scattered across the map, a boat, a bike, and a flying fossil to ride. Pick a companion from your yard and it follows you around, sniffing out finds and making your kind of digging pay better.

✨ Features

  • Infinite, hand-tuned procedural world — 6 biomes, 66 species
  • A walkable Museum with six themed rooms, plus amber cases as a second collection
  • 3D voxel skeletons you complete piece by piece
  • Awaken extinct species, then breed chimeras of your own
  • A house to furnish — 12 themes, 250+ hand-drawn pieces, wallpaper and floors
  • A yard your creatures live in, and a companion that follows you around
  • Treasure maps, caves, wonders, day/night cycle and seasons
  • Cozy and low-pressure by design — no combat, no game over
  • Plays in your browser · installable · works offline (PWA)

🎮 Controls

Move: WASD / arrow keys / on-screen joystick  ·  Interact & dig: E or the on-screen A button  ·  Fossil Book: L  ·  Bag: I  ·  Map: M

🌍 Languages

English · Italiano · Русский
